## Audit before rewriting

Perform a complete narrative and visual audit of the existing corpus inventory.
Verify the actual inventory within that repository; do not assume quantities or
acceptance status from a platform example. Produce a cost/dependency map and
recommendations, not modified lessons or replacement artwork.

Track four **independent** statuses: PEDAGOGY, LANGUAGE, NARRATIVE and VISUAL.
Previously Owner-accepted pedagogy/language remain accepted; Bible adoption does
not revoke them. Narrative is REASSESS and visual is RESET REQUIRED until the new
audit/review resolves them. This is a direction for the corpus operator to verify
and record there, not evidence that an audit happened here.

Do not give final Owner acceptance to pending corpus material before its narrative
audit against the Bible. Do not change an accepted lesson merely to satisfy new
art direction without its own Owner review.

Use independent recommendation maps:

- Narrative: **KEEP / ENRICH / REFRAME / REWRITE**.
- Visual: **KEEP / REINTERPRET / REPLACE**.

For each recommendation assess comprehension, vocabulary/repetition, recurring
entities, temporal/narrative continuity, world expansion, folklore pacing, visual
disambiguation, rendering needs and implementation cost. Pedagogy wins when
spectacle conflicts. Preserve clear separation between a proposed map, approved
changes and implemented/accepted results.

## Mandatory visual reset rule

Current corpus visual assets are evidence of past implementation, not design
precedent. Do not preserve their composition, illustration style, prompt structure,
palette, framing, character construction or scene grammar merely for consistency.
Consistency with legacy artwork is explicitly NOT a goal. Derive the new visual
system from the approved World & Experience Bible and new Product Owner-approved
visual benchmarks. Reuse an existing visual decision only when it independently
survives the new art-direction review.

Keep private narrative/character/location/arc/folklore progression documents and
visual production/reset/entity registry/continuity documents **in the private
corpus only**. Neither their contents nor prompts/real references/private QA belong
in the platform, public PRs, logs or CI artifacts. Actual renderer-specific identity
records remain private; any public Experience Contract receives only independently
reviewed generic semantics and synthetic examples.

Report audit findings and migration cost to Owner privately. Stop before rewriting
accepted lessons, replacing production assets, altering Contract v1, publishing
materials or performing any action outside that corpus session's authorization.
Only Owner-approved public-safe outputs may return through a controlled handoff.
