# 0016 — Owner-controlled product-world authority and verified public copies

Status: Accepted engineering decision within Owner-authorized Bible adoption
Date: 2026-10-13

## Context

Owner introduced World & Experience Bible v1.0 as normative **public-safe** direction
across platform/corpus/narrative/visual production. Private Master Specification,
real content and production references remain confidential. Implementations must
share authority without opening private repository access or treating current
assets as precedent.

## Decision

Owner owns product-world direction. Record the canonical public v1.0 document in
docs/product-world with an exact version/SHA-256 record. A separately authorized
corpus operator installs a byte-identical copy; platform access is not needed.
Bootstrap checks verify working and indexed bytes. Compare only explicitly
supplied authorized public exports; never discover or mount the private repository.
Report corpus synchronization as pending until separately evidenced.

The Bible governs conflicting visual/world direction; accepted security/privacy,
pedagogy and domain semantics remain intact. Past studies/assets/evidence are
provisional and require independent review, not cosmetic preservation or automatic
replacement. Separate pedagogy/language acceptance from narrative/visual audit.

Experience Contract is a future approved public semantic boundary. Its schema and
Course Package compatibility are not yet defined. No current receiver allowlist,
Contract v1, API, migration or renderer dependency changes. RetroCel remains a
future candidate subject to visual/continuity acceptance. No additional phase or
full visual rollout is authorized.

## Alternatives

A corpus-owned private Bible would prevent public engineering continuation.
Cross-repository symlinks, mounts or direct reads violate the accepted boundary.
Unversioned copies drift silently. Inventing Experience Contract fields now would
replace missing bilateral semantics with implementation guesses.

## Consequences

Explicit Owner-approved versions/hashes and coordinated acknowledgments are
required. A checksum verifies byte identity, not external repository installation
or product acceptance. Current handoff tooling rejects unsupported formats; future
Bible/Experience handoff needs a separately reviewed format/receiver change.
Existing production direction and deferred gates remain; no past acceptance record
is rewritten. The private corpus audit is a separate task, not performed here.
